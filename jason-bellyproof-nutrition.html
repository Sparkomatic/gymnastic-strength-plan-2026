How to use
Two meals a day on a 7-day rotation — Meal 1 is your post-workout feed, Meal 2 is dinner. Days alternate vegetarian and pescatarian. Each day's macros land near ~60P / 88C / 53F; where a day runs low on fat, use the top-up tip in the footer (avocado, nuts, or extra oil) to hit the target. Weights are for the stated form (cooked / drained where noted).
