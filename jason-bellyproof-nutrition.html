How to use
Two meals a day on a 7-day rotation — Meal 1 is your post-workout feed, Meal 2 is dinner. Days alternate vegetarian and pescatarian. Following the BellyProof guidance, these are grain-free apart from rice (no bread, wraps, bagels, oats, quinoa, pasta or noodles other than rice) and use no milk — yogurt and cheese stay as the protein anchors. Each day's carbs are held to ≤80g with protein near ~60g; where a day runs low on fat, use the top-up tip in the footer (avocado, nuts, or extra oil). Weights are for the stated form (cooked / drained where noted).

Reminder: macros are approximate targets, not exact prescriptions — brands and portion sizes vary. These days are grain-free apart from rice and use no milk (yogurt & cheese kept); carbs are held to ≤80g/day. Meal 1 lands in the post-workout window; get the protein in first. On any day that reads low on fat, use the top-up tip. Keep hydration up across the eating window.

Veg swap: every meal that uses fish or shellfish (Days 2, 4 & 6) has a fully vegetarian alternative underneath it, built to land on the same protein/carb/fat numbers — swap in whichever meal you prefer, no need to touch the rest of the day. Most swaps use seitan as the lean, high-protein stand-in for fish; if you're avoiding gluten, sub in an equal weight of pressed extra-firm tofu or tempeh instead — expect fat to run a little higher and adjust the oil in the recipe down slightly to compensate.
