How the BellyProof method works — read this first

Every session is a fasted, circuit-style burn that hits the same fat zones in order — belly (front) → left love handle → right love handle → a secondary target. The logic per zone runs in two beats: first break the fat loose, then burn it off. You move back-to-back with no rest inside a cycle — treat the end of each cycle as your "hit the buzzer" moment.

1 · HOLD — isometric rebound, 45–60s (squeezes blood out of the zone)
2 · BREATH — ~20s breath hold at the end of the hold (the priming trick)
3 · EXPLODE — 15–25s all-out burst (floods the primed tissue)
4 · SINK — 30–60s slow, grinding core work (the "clearance")

The breath hold sits inside the HOLD row — finish the isometric, hold your breath ~20s, then release straight into the explosive move so the "blood rebound" lands while the zone is primed.

Practical rules: train fasted, mid-morning, no food before. First meal 1–2 hours after (protein + carbs on training days) so the freed fat isn't re-stored. Sessions run ~45–50 min. Most moves below are ones you already do in your main plan — the method is about sequencing, not learning new lifts.

Phase 1 · ~16 min
Prime & Desensitize
Cycle every zone once with short holds + short bursts to wake up the stubborn fat. Ends with "The Reset" — mobility + box breathing.
Phase 2 · ~14 min
Attack
Longer holds (50–60s) + the biggest explosive moves while each zone is primed. This is the hardest block — the surge.
Phase 3 · ~16 min
Scoop & Clear
Steady, lighter, large-muscle work that uses big muscles as the "sink" to actually burn off the freed fat.
Cool down · 5–20 min
Mop Up
Neck reset, scapular circles, then optional "mindless cardio" — steady and easy for a bonus fat-burn.
