How these circuit workouts run

Four fasted workouts plus one active-recovery day. Each workout is 10 numbered SETS — do the moves in a set back-to-back (no rest inside a set), then take the stated recovery before the next. The session alternates between two feels, and every workout finishes with steady cardio (or Turkish get-ups):

Hard sets
The explosive / compound circuits — jumps, swings, split squats, pull-ups. Push until you're breathless, slightly buzzed, skin warming up. These repeat 3–4× through the session. Chase that feeling.
Steady sets
The skill / core / mobility grind in between — medium effort but more sweat and harder to concentrate. Slower, controlled, deliberate.

Rules that carry through: train fasted; the Heat Protocol (Rub-a-Blubber) goes mid-workout where marked; drink water at the checkpoints; warm-up is listed at the top of each workout.

Personal digitisation of your own BellyProof Stages 2 & 3 printout, following its structure and intent. The program's proprietary move names (Reverse Frog, Surprised Kitty, Humpty Dumpty, Papera, etc.) have been swapped for common exercises that train the same thing — so the pattern is intact but every move is one you'll recognise.
