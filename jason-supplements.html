Creatine Monohydrate 5g daily Training
~£3–4/month Creapure® — bulk.com →
Why you're taking it
Strongest evidence base of any training supplement. At 51, creatine offsets the natural age-related decline in muscle phosphocreatine stores — supporting explosive power output, strength, and recovery between sets in gymnastic training. Also has emerging evidence for cardiovascular and cognitive protection.
Why this product
Genuine Creapure® badge — German-manufactured, 99.99% pure, no fillers. Unbranded creatine often contains impurities (creatinine, dicyandiamide). Cheapest per gram of any major UK retailer. 500g lasts ~100 days at 5g/day.
Omega-3 Fish Oil (PROM3GA) 6 caps/day · 2–3g EPA+DHA Training + Health
£17–34/month PROM3GA — bulk.com →
Why you're taking it
Your CRP-hs of 6.9 is the most urgent marker on your panel — more than double the lab upper limit of 3.0. EPA+DHA at therapeutic doses (2–3g/day) directly reduces CRP and fibrinogen by suppressing prostaglandin inflammatory pathways. Also raises HDL (yours at 51, target 55–85) and supports joint and connective tissue recovery.
Why this product
Standard "1,000mg fish oil" at H&B contains only ~300mg actual EPA+DHA — you'd need 7–10 capsules to hit the target. PROM3GA delivers 1,800mg per 3-cap serving — the only Informed Sport-certified fish oil across all major UK retailers. Budget: Super Strength Omega 3 (4–5 caps/day, ~£16–20/month, no certification).
Magnesium Bisglycinate 300–400mg elemental · before bed Training + Health
~£4/month Magnesium Bisglycinate — bulk.com →
Why you're taking it
Depleted heavily by training (lost in sweat, consumed in energy metabolism). Supports sleep quality and muscle recovery, and has evidence for lowering CRP — directly relevant to your elevated inflammatory markers. Also a cofactor in the methylation cycle, supporting the homocysteine picture.
Why this product
Bisglycinate form only — magnesium oxide (most cheap products) absorbs at ~4%. Magnesium citrate causes loose stools at higher doses. H&B own-brand magnesium glycinate costs ~£30/month for the same dose — bulk.com is 7× cheaper. Take 3–4 tablets before bed to reach 300–400mg elemental magnesium.
Healthspan — also order Opti-Turmeric and Milk Thistle here (Tier 3) to consolidate into one delivery
Vitamin D3 + K2 4,000 IU D3 · 100µg K2-MK7 Health
~£7/month D3 & K2 — Healthspan →
Why you're taking it
Your TGA antibody of 16 vs lab range 0–0.9 is the most under-discussed finding on your panel — suggesting possible autoimmune thyroid activity. Low D3 is strongly associated with Hashimoto's and D3 is a key immune modulator relevant to your elevated monocytes and eosinophils. Essential Oct–Apr for anyone in the UK (no meaningful UVB from October through April).
Why this product
4,000 IU D3 (cholecalciferol ✓) + 100µg K2 as MK-7 (menaquinone-7 ✓). bulk.com's K2 is only 75µg — below the 100µg target. K2 directs calcium into bone rather than soft tissue — don't take high-dose D3 without it. MK-7 has a much longer half-life than MK-4 (the inferior form in cheaper products).
Tier 2 — Targeted Support
Connective tissue · adrenal / cortisol · immune · ~£24–28/month
bulk.com — add to your Tier 1 bulk.com order
Collagen Peptides + Vit C 15g · 30–60 min pre-session Training
£13–14/month Collagen & Vit C — bulk.com →
Why you're taking it
Gymnastic strength training places high mechanical load on tendons, ligaments, and fascia — the structures most prone to injury and slowest to adapt. Collagen synthesis peaks when you load 30–60 minutes after a priming dose. Entirely preventive and performance-targeted; no blood marker flags this directly.
Why this product
Must be hydrolysed bovine collagen (Type I & III ✓) — intact collagen passes through undigested. The vitamin C at 24mg per serving is below the 50mg+ target for optimal collagen synthesis — add a cheap vit C tablet alongside, or squeeze half a lemon into the water when you take it. Don't buy collagen tablets — you can't economically reach 15g/day.
Ashwagandha (KSM-66) 600mg/day · with food Training + Health
£8–10/month KSM-66® — bulk.com →
Why you're taking it
Your DUTCH test flagged low cortisol and low-normal DHEA-S — consistent with HPA axis dysregulation. KSM-66 is the functional replacement for Vital Adapt from the original prescription, and has the strongest RCT evidence of any adaptogen for lowering cortisol, supporting adrenal function, and improving sleep. Multiple RCTs also show significant improvements in VO₂ max, strength, and recovery — directly training-relevant. Works synergistically with DHEA (Tier 3) when you add that post-retest.
Why this product
KSM-66 only — root-only extract, standardised to 5% withanolides, with the most clinical evidence of any ashwagandha form. Avoid "root and leaf" blends or products without a stated withanolide percentage. 300mg twice daily (morning + dinner) or 600mg once with dinner. bulk.com uses the branded KSM-66 extract.
Holland & Barrett — also order Beef Liver and Milk Thistle here (Tier 3) to consolidate into one delivery
Zinc Picolinate (Solgar) 22mg · with food Training + Health
£3–4/month Solgar Zinc Picolinate — H&B →
Why you're taking it
Your WBC of 3.5 sits at the bottom of lab range and below the functional optimum (5.0–8.0). Elevated monocytes (8.4) and eosinophils (4.6) both point to immune dysregulation. Zinc is essential for immune cell production, testosterone synthesis, and thyroid hormone conversion — all relevant to your panel. Heavy training further depletes it.
Why this product
The one supplement that must come from H&B. bulk.com uses zinc monomethionine, Myprotein uses zinc citrate — both decent but picolinate has the strongest absorption evidence. Solgar is a premium, independently tested brand. 22mg is within the optimal 15–25mg range. 100 tablets ≈ 3 months' supply. Don't exceed 40mg/day — suppresses copper absorption.
Tier 3 — Therapeutic & Functional Health
Address specific markers from your blood panel · 4 suppliers · ~£55–75/month
Cytoplan — active methylation forms not available at bulk.com, H&B, or Healthspan
Methylation Complex (Methyl Factors) 1 capsule/day · permanent Health
~£13/month Methyl Factors — Cytoplan →
Why you're taking it
Your homocysteine of 8.0 µmol/L is above the functional optimum of 7.0 and is the most credible single root cause connecting multiple markers on your panel. Elevated homocysteine is directly toxic to blood vessel walls, triggering the cascade that raises CRP (6.9), fibrinogen, ESR, monocytes, and eosinophils. Your MTHFR variant reduces the ability to convert standard folate to active 5-MTHF — supplementing with pre-methylated active forms bypasses the genetic block entirely. MTHFR is genetic — this stays in the stack permanently.
Why this product
Active forms only: 5-MTHF (not folic acid), methylcobalamin (not cyanocobalamin), P5P (B6), TMG, and critically riboflavin-5-phosphate (B2) — a direct MTHFR enzyme cofactor missing from most competitors including the previous Metagenics product. bulk.com, Myprotein, and H&B all use inactive forms that can't address the underlying impairment regardless of dose. Already switched from Metagenics — Cytoplan is more complete and cheaper.
Healthspan — add to your Tier 1 Healthspan order · both situational (drop once markers normalise at retest)
Curcumin (Opti-Turmeric) daily · with food · situational Health
~£5–6/month Opti-Turmeric® — Healthspan →
Why you're taking it
Your CRP-hs of 6.9 is more than double the lab upper limit of 3.0. Curcumin has the strongest RCT evidence of any supplement for reducing CRP — multiple trials show meaningful reductions by inhibiting NF-κB, a key inflammatory signalling pathway. Works via a different mechanism to omega-3, so the two are complementary. Drop once CRP normalises at retest.
Why this product
Plain curcumin has near-zero bioavailability — it passes through almost unabsorbed. Most products add piperine (black pepper) as a workaround. Healthspan's Opti-Turmeric uses a liquid curcumin delivery system achieving 185× better absorption than standard turmeric powder — no piperine needed. Add to the same order as D3+K2.
Milk Thistle 1–2 caps/day · with food · situational Health
£4–5/month Milk Thistle — Healthspan →
Why you're taking it
Your ALT of 33 is above the functional range of 0–26 — a mild liver enzyme elevation. Silymarin (the active compound) has solid evidence for reducing elevated liver enzymes and protecting hepatocytes. Also improves bile flow, which increases absorption of all the fat-soluble supplements in your stack (D3, K2, vitamin A, omega-3). Drop once ALT normalises at retest.
Why this product
Look for a product standardised to 80% silymarin — the form used in clinical trials. bulk.com previously stocked this but no longer lists it. Healthspan carry it — consolidate into the same order as D3+K2 and Opti-Turmeric. Milk thistle + curcumin is a well-established liver/inflammation combination; they're complementary, not duplicating.
Holland & Barrett — add to your Tier 2 H&B zinc order
Desiccated Beef Liver 4 caps/day · natural vitamin A Health
£20–30/month Grass-fed beef liver — H&B / Amazon →
Why you're taking it
Replaces EVCLO (fermented cod liver oil) from the original prescription. EVCLO's value was natural preformed vitamin A (retinol) — not its EPA+DHA, which PROM3GA covers. Beef liver is the densest retinol source in existence, and also provides natural B12, folate, iron, and CoQ10. Vitamin A supports thyroid function — directly relevant to your borderline TSH and TGA antibody of 16. Milk thistle in your stack provides a hepatoprotective layer alongside the supplemental vitamin A.
Why this product
Look for grass-fed, freeze-dried — brands like Wilderland or Ancestral Supplements. Freeze-drying preserves the nutrient profile without heat damage. Avoid proprietary blends with no per-capsule dose transparency. Cheaper alternative: 100g lamb or beef liver once a week costs ~£1–2, is more bioavailable than any capsule, and achieves the same outcome. Don't exceed 4 caps/day to keep retinol within a safe range.

DHEA 25mg morning · with food Training + Health Start after hormone retest only
£10–15/month Life Extension DHEA 25mg →
Why you're taking it
Your DUTCH test directly prescribed DHEA because it flagged low cortisol and low-normal DHEA-S. The Report of Findings notes this affects energy, recovery from exercise, muscle building, and mood — all directly relevant to gymnastic training. The most significant gap from the original Evolution Rehab prescription. Works synergistically with ashwagandha for the adrenal picture. Retest DHEA-S at Medical Diagnostics first — DHEA is a hormone precursor that converts to testosterone and oestrogen, and starting blind risks overshooting.
Why this product
Start at 25mg — not the 50mg most products default to, which is too high to start blind. Life Extension is rigorously third-party tested with established formulations. Not stocked by bulk.com, H&B, Healthspan, or Cytoplan — Amazon UK is the most reliable route. Reassess dose after 8–12 weeks and retest DHEA-S at ~3 months.
Glutathione (Liposomal) 500mg/day Health Hold — add only if CRP/ALT don't respond at retest
£20–30/month Liposomal glutathione — Amazon →
Why you'd take it
Glutathione is the body's primary intracellular antioxidant and the liver's main detoxification molecule — directly relevant to elevated CRP, monocytes, eosinophils, and ALT. Was in the original Evolution Rehab prescription. With curcumin, omega-3, and milk thistle already addressing these markers, assess at retest whether they were sufficient before adding the cost.
Why liposomal form only
Standard oral glutathione is broken down in the gut before reaching the bloodstream — largely ineffective. Liposomal or S-acetyl glutathione only. Budget alternative: NAC (N-acetyl cysteine) 600mg at ~£8–12/month raises glutathione indirectly by supplying the rate-limiting precursor.
